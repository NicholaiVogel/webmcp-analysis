# FROZEN CATEGORY TAXONOMY (pre-registered 2026-09-05, before Stage 1)

Reviewers assign exactly one PRIMARY category from this list, plus up to one optional
secondary `category_secondary` from the same list. Playful, artistic, and experimental
projects have real homes here — do NOT default them to `other`.

- game — playable games, interactive fiction, game-like experiences
- music-audio — music creation, DAWs, synths, audio tools, podcasts
- creative-art — visual art, design tools, animation, video, storytelling (non-game)
- dev-tool — developer tooling, code, testing, deployment, API/MCP infrastructure UX
- agent-infra — infrastructure whose product IS agent integration: tool registries,
  WebMCP frameworks/orchestration layers, agent dashboards beyond a single app
- productivity — tasks, notes, docs, email, calendars, personal workflow
- business-crm — CRM, sales, marketing, operations, B2B SaaS
- commerce — shopping, storefronts, marketplaces, payments
- data-viz — analytics, dashboards, visualization, reporting
- education — learning, tutoring, study, reference
- research — literature review, knowledge exploration, scientific workflows
- writing — prose generation/editing, blogs, journalism (primary purpose writing)
- social — social networking, community, messaging-focused experiences
- health — health, fitness, mental wellness, medical
- finance — personal or business finance, budgeting, investing
- travel-maps — travel planning, maps, local discovery
- other — anything genuinely not placeable above (expected < 5% of projects)

Rules:
- Category is contextual metadata for funnel fairness (category leaders lane) and
  reporting. It NEVER multiplies or adjusts scores.
- When torn, choose the category describing what the USER primarily does with the
  product, not the technology used.
