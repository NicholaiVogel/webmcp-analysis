I took the decompiled works of pokemon emerald (pokeemerald-decomp), and asked codex to reimplement it in JS. I then asked it to make a parity list, and thought about what I want to preserve and what I'd like to change. \n\n Challenges we ran into \n\n making the map feel responsive was a big issue, especially when zooming out. I also had to match the exact ticks that a gameboy uses, because I want the site to feel realistic. \n\n Accomplishments that we're proud of \n\n I'm really happy with how the zoomed out view looks, and the fact that you can still walk around and explore the region whilst having a "god view". \n\n What we learned \n\n webmcp in my opinion is currently constrained, I look forward to contributing to the standard. it needs bidirectional communication - the website needs to have a callback / some way to tell the agent when a task is done or ready instead of just polling. \n\n What's next for overworld-webmcp \n\n I'd love to make it multiplayer, and add secret bases. I might add battling capabilities as well, but it would be okay to leave it out since there are plenty of battle simulators, and this is more of an artistic project instead of a faithful simulation of the game. \n\n \n\n \n Built With \n\n audioworklet bun cloudflare cloudflare-pages html5 javascript python stylex typescript webassembly webmcp \n \n\n \n Try it out \n\n \n \n \n pmanot.com \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n \n \n Submitted to\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n The WebMCP Challenge \n \n\n\n \n\n\n \n\n \n \n\n \n \n Created by\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n Purav Manot \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n Like\n \n \n\n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n Share this project:\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n \n \n \n Updates \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n Purav Manot \n started this project\n \n —\n \n a day ago \n \n \n\n
