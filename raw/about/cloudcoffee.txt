\n \n \n \n\n \n \n Build truthful, role-specific resumes with structured content, ATS guidance, and a live preview. \n \n 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 \n\n\n\n \n Inspiration \n\n \n People do many projects, jobs, hackathons, and achievements, but cannot fit everything into one resume. \n Every job needs a different version of your resume based on the role. \n Tailoring resumes manually for every application takes too much time. \n \n\n What it does \n\n \n Stores your complete career context: projects, achievements, skills, work experience, and metrics. \n Uses the job description to generate a tailored resume and check ATS relevance. \n Tracks applications, replies, interviews, and rejections in one place. \n \n\n How we built it \n\n \n Built CloudCoffee around WebMCP so AI agents like Codex can interact with the application. \n The agent can read career context, create resumes, and manage applications. \n The web app provides resume, ATS, career, and application management. \n \n\n Challenges we ran into \n\n \n Structuring messy career information so AI can understand it. \n Finding the right experience for a job instead of simply matching keywords. \n Designing WebMCP tools that AI agents can use reliably. \n \n\n Accomplishments that we're proud of \n\n \n Built more than a normal AI resume generator by storing the user's full career context. \n Connected the workflow with AI agents through WebMCP. \n Combined resume generation, ATS checking, and application tracking in one product. \n \n\n What we learned \n\n \n AI becomes much more useful when it remembers your previous work and achievements. \n Most people have valuable career experiences that never appear on their resume. \n Structured career context helps AI create much better job-specific resumes. \n \n\n What's next for CloudCoffee \n\n \n Help users automatically look for jobs, tailor resume and cover letter and apply for jobs automatically. \n Improve ATS analysis, interview preparation, and
