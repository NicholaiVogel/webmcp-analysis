Drop any waiver, consent document or agreement into ChatGPT or Chrome and it becomes a live, legally signable e-sign form. Like Comment Story Updates Inspiration To convert a PDF into a signable form, you have to retype every field. We wanted it to be a single sentence to your browser: "Make this a signable form." What it does Two WebMCP tools on formesign.com : create_form turns your agent's submitted document into a live signable form within your account, and drops you into the signing preview. edit_full_form allows editing in natural language and seeing the changes as they appear on the form. How we built it Form generation: our document-to-form model, fine-tuned on OpenAI models, converts your agent's HTML into the full form: fields, agreement text, signature blocks. WebMCP layer: exposes only the tools that you need on the page you are at, always acting as the logged-in user. Challenges we ran into Sign in. Forms are in your account, but our homepage has no auth state at all. Our first attempt was a separate get_login_status tool that the agent had to call before anything else. It felt clumsy, so we scrapped it: now create_form checks the sign-in itself, and if you are not logged in, it just redirects the tab to Google sign-in and the agent picks up from where it left off. Accomplishments that we're proud of Onboarding has gone from 15-20 minutes of uploading, reviewing and clicking to under a minute of conversation. The user only does about 5% of the work: describe, sign in, review. What we learned With WebMCP, the page dictates which tools exist. On the homepage we offer to create a form; within the editor we offer to edit it. The agent's world always matches your screen. What's next for Formesign WebMCP: Document to Signature Form Reporting and onboarding tools. Ask "who has signed so far?" in your browser, or go from landing page to first signed document in one conversation.
