Users repeatedly reconstruct that context before taking action. \n\n What it does \n\n NYVARA explores how WebMCP can turn websites into agent-ready environments. It connects contextual understanding with structured web actions, helping an AI agent identify what needs attention, what is waiting on someone else, and what action can be taken next. \n\n How we built it \n\n We designed NYVARA around contextual intelligence, persistent workflows, cases/folders, action states, and human-controlled decision making. WebMCP provides a way to expose relevant web actions directly to an AI agent. \n\n Challenges \n\n The main challenge is preserving enough context for useful agent actions while keeping the user in control and avoiding unnecessary or incorrect actions. \n\n What we learned \n\n AI agents become more useful when they understand not only what tools are available, but why and when an action should be taken. \n\n What's next \n\n NYVARA will expand this approach across business and personal workflows, communications, scheduling, CRM, and other web-based tools. \n\n What it does \n\n How we built it \n\n Challenges we ran into \n\n Accomplishments that we're proud of \n\n What we learned \n\n What's next for NYVARA \n\n \n\n \n Built With \n\n ai chatgpt css html javascript openai webmcp \n \n\n \n Try it out \n\n \n \n \n nyvara-webmcp-chfa1ep0k-direccioncvrm-1346.vercel.app \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n Created by\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n ARTURO AGUIRRE \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n Like\n \n \n\n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n Share this project:\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n \n \n \n Updates \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n ARTURO AGUIRRE \n started this project\n \n —\n \n a day ago \n \n \n\n
