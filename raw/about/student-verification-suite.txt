The agent can discover requirements, retrieve appropriate evidence from an authorized document vault, submit it, monitor verification status, recover from rejected documents, and return to the human only when approval is required. \n\n This prototype simulates multiple student-verification providers and merchant offers to demonstrate how an agent-native verification ecosystem could work. The verification provider remains the authority; the agent operates the workflow, while the human stays in control of sensitive actions. \n\n The goal is not to replace verification providers. It is to replace the repetitive human-operated workflow around verification. \n\n \n\n \n Built With \n\n codex react tailwind-css typescript vite webmcp \n \n\n \n Try it out \n\n \n \n \n web-mcp-three.vercel.app \n \n \n \n GitHub Repo \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n \n \n Submitted to\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n The WebMCP Challenge \n \n\n\n \n\n\n \n\n \n \n\n \n \n Created by\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n vudattu maniteja \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n Like\n \n \n\n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n Share this project:\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n \n \n \n Updates \n\n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n Devpost \n \n About \n Careers \n Contact \n Help \n \n \n \n\n \n \n
