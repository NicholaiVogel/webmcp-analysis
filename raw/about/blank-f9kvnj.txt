Scan the QR on the desktop (or open the same room URL). Choose videos on the phone; one fills the canvas, more split into a grid. Play, pause, and volume stay on the phone. Read-only social timelines can sit on the same canvas. \n\n Why WebMCP \n\n The canvas page registers named tools on document.modelContext . An agent can mount videos and feeds from those tools instead of scraping HTML. The public demo does not need the optional extension. \n\n On boot, document.modelContext.registerTool for: \n\n \n youtube.get_current_video , youtube.get_playback_state , youtube.pause_source , youtube.get_visible_recommendations \n social.get_visible_feed , social.search_visible_context , social.refresh \n canvas.create_workspace , canvas.mount_youtube , canvas.render_social_feed , canvas.set_layout \n canvas.player.play , canvas.player.pause , canvas.player.seek_relative , canvas.player.seek_absolute , canvas.player.set_volume , canvas.player.get_state \n canvas.panel.focus , canvas.panel.resize , canvas.panel.hide , canvas.panel.remove , canvas.get_state , canvas.clear , canvas.open_x \n \n\n If modelContext is missing, the canvas still runs locally. Judges can try it in ChatGPT’s in-app browser or Chrome 149+ with chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing . \n\n How people and agents work together \n\n The person stays on the phone. The page (and an agent, when WebMCP is on) composes the PC from tools. Previously this needed a custom dashboard for each layout. \n\n Public path \n\n \n Live: https://blank-webmcp-canvas.pages.dev \n Repo (MIT): https://github.com/0809android/webmcp-blank-canvas \n Demo: https://youtu.be/gKjVJ0RrwEk \n \n\n YouTube playback uses the official public embed.
