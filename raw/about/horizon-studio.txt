Creative media production is your AI’s new skill Like Comment Story Updates Horizon Studio - Your AIs new creative skill Inspiration We believe technology should be accessible to everyone, for every idea, regardless of technical experience. Creative media has always required a wide range of skills and years of practice. WebMCP is helping change that. It gives AI access to the tools needed to help bring a person’s vision to life. What it does Horizon Studio is a browser-based multimedia suite for creating interactive sites, presentations, animation, 3D experiences, and video. Users provide the creative director. Through WebMCP, the AI can inspect the project, build scenes, animate objects, direct cameras, adjust materials, and prepare the final experience for sharing. How we built it Horizon runs entirely in the browser using TypeScript, Three.js, WebGL, WebGPU, and native browser media tools. The editor, runtime, animation system, publisher, and WebMCP tools all work with the same project model. Human and AI changes share the same history, validation, undo, and redo system. Projects can be exported as portable .hzn files or published as standalone HTML, CSS, and JavaScript experiences. Challenges we ran into The greatest challenge was providing professional creative depth without forcing people to become experts first. We also had to make AI changes clear, safe, and reversible while working within the media and rendering limits of different browsers. Accomplishments that we’re proud of WebMCP gives the AI access to Horizon’s actual creative tools. It can work directly with the same objects, cameras, materials, and timelines as the person using the Studio. Our competition presentation was also created as a Horizon project, edited through WebMCP, and published with Horizon’s own runtime. What we learned People don’t need to know the technical name for every effect they imagine. They need a way to describe their vision, see the result, and continue directing it. WebMCP helps turn AI from an advisor into a capable creative collaborator. What’s next for Horizon Studio We plan to expand the video and audio workspace, add deeper scene and camera tools, improve accessibility, and give AI access to more of the production process. The goal remains the same: put powerful creative tools within reach of anyone with an idea.
