Commonly, people just want "that thing" to work, right now. WebMCP feels like the magical layer we've been missing! \n\n What it does \n\n Batch Relay allows you to visualize and edit prints, text and image nodes, before you "add to cart". You can also interactively say use "that" image and order "that" print, all in the background. It lets us have a sort of companion-like shopping experience0 This proof-of-concept demo, brings an agentic layer into the interface with WebMCP. Anything a human can do, an agent has access to. \n\n How we built it \n\n This project was built using Codex, ChatGPT, Vercel/Next.js, and a bit of other LLMs sprinkled in. I throughly tested it with the built-in ChatGPT web Browser as well as Nekuda's WebMCP workbench Chrome plugin. \n\n Challenges we ran into \n\n Testing the interactions were a bit of a challenge. Sometime's the tools would break down, and would need new chat threads, be told how to proceed and such. Outside of that, it was clear Batch Relay's API needed some other stuff, so a bit of back and forth here! \n\n Accomplishments that we're proud of \n\n With new technology, comes new challenges. As such, I'm personally proud of the special agent-human interactions I crafted, such as the live previews that show up in the lower left corner, asking a human to confirm or deny a change made by an agent. This also lets humans stay in-focus with their current task at hand. It's there when you need it with a gentle nudge, and gone when you don't. \n\n You can also see what an agent is working on, in the lower right corner. Actions it takes will be presented here, so it's not a total "blackbox" experience! \n\n What we learned \n\n I honestly learned a lot, but most importantly, this challenge has shifted my product-thinking. Going forward I will lead with not only thinking about the user experience, the agentic experience, but also the human-agent layer. \n\n What's next for Batch Relay WebMCP \n\n A lot! We've already rolled some of this into our custom Templating Engine, as well as our Form builder. It's clear WebMCP is here to change how we interact with our browsers and the way we experience the web... How exciting! As such, I'm excited to keep building & exploring new ways to surface even more of the platform! \n\n \n\n \n Built With \n\n codex vercel \n \n\n \n Try it out \n\n \n \n \n webmcp.batchrelay.com \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n \n \n Submitted to\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n The WebMCP Challenge \n \n\n\n \n\n\n \n\n \n \n\n \n \n Created by\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n SENTINELITE Layton \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n Perfectionist trying to perfect imperfection. \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n Like\n \n \n\n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n Share this project:\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n \n \n \n Updates \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n SENTINELITE Layton \n started this project\n \n —\n \n a day ago \n \n \n\n
