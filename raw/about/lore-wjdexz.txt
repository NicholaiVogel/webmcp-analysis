A next pointer in every return, a worklist of gaps, and pedagogy warnings like "wall of text" or "no visual block" improved course quality more than any amount of description text. Progressive disclosure matters for models too: paint the roadmap first, then fill it in. And the best place for a model in a learning app might be outside the app entirely, in the conversation the learner already has open. \n\n What's next for Lore \n\n \n Agent-authored podcast episodes played by the built-in speech engine (the tool already exists; the player needs a proper "listen" mode). \n Two-way tutoring \n Longer courses that evolve: insert_lesson driven by weak concepts in get_progress_report, so the agent keeps writing where you keep missing. \n Import and export of a course as a single file, so a course one agent wrote can be shared with another learner. \n Testing with more WebMCP clients as they appear, and a proper mobile pass. \n \n\n \n\n \n Built With \n\n vite zod \n \n\n \n Try it out \n\n \n \n \n GitHub Repo \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n \n \n Submitted to\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n The WebMCP Challenge \n \n\n\n \n\n\n \n\n \n \n\n \n \n Created by\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n Toprak Tarkan Dikici \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n Like\n \n \n\n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n Share this project:\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n \n \n \n Updates \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n Toprak Tarkan Dikici \n started this project\n \n —\n \n a day ago \n \n \n\n
