A single new medicine can introduce an interaction that's easy to miss.\nWe built SafeDose AI — RegimenGuard to turn complex medication information into understandable safety insights — and with WebMCP , allow AI agents to work directly with structured medication context. \n\n \n The agent investigates. SafeDose provides context. The human decides. \n\n 🛡️ What it does \n\n SafeDose is an agent-native medication safety workspace that helps users: \n\n \n 💊 Manage their current medication regimen \n ⚠️ Detect potential drug interactions \n 📊 Understand an overall regimen safety score \n 🔴 Distinguish critical risks from cautions and safer combinations \n 🔎 Search structured medication information \n 👥 Support caregiver and medication-management workflows \n \n \n\n 🤖 Powered by WebMCP \n\n SafeDose exposes three focused, read-only agent tools :\n search_medication 🔎 — Find medication information\n get_current_regimen 💊 — Understand the user's current regimen\n check_regimen_safety 🛡️ — Check a candidate medication against that regimen \n\n Instead of repeatedly copying medication lists into an AI conversation, compatible agents can work with SafeDose's structured capabilities directly. \n\n Agents investigate — they don't prescribe or modify medications. \n\n 🛠️ How we built it \n\n SafeDose combines:\n React + TypeScript ⚛️ → application \n WebMCP 🤖 → agent-native tools \n Medication/FDA data 💊 → drug information \n Google AI Studio ✨ → AI-assisted development \n GitHub 🐙 → source control \n Vercel ▲ → live deployment \n\n We also built an Agent Activity interface to make WebMCP capabilities visible and understandable. \n\n 🧩 Challenges we ran into \n\n Our biggest challenge was bridging two worlds:\n Human UI ↔ WebMCP ↔ AI Agent \nWe worked through evolving WebMCP browser APIs, tool registration, deployment, agent discovery, and medication-data integration. \n\n Healthcare also raised an important design question: How much authority should an AI agent have? \nOur answer was deliberate — read-only tools and human-controlled decisions. \n\n 🏆 Accomplishments that we're proud of \n\n We're especially proud that SafeDose isn't simply “another healthcare chatbot.” \nWe transformed an existing medication-safety experience into an agent-native web application , while preserving a polished interface for traditional users. \n\n Most importantly, WebMCP addresses a genuinely contextual problem: \n\n \n It's not only “What does this medication do?” \nIt's “What does this medication mean alongside everything I'm already taking?” \n \n\n 🧠 What we learned \n\n WebMCP showed us that the future of AI on the web isn't necessarily putting a chatbot on every website. \n\n It's about making websites themselves understandable and actionable to agents. \nAnd in healthcare, powerful AI doesn't always mean maximum autonomy.\n Good agents also know where their authority ends. \n\n 🚀 What's next for SafeDose AI — RegimenGuard \n\n Next, we envision:\n💊 Medication reconciliation \n👨‍⚕️ Pharmacist-review workflows \n👨‍👩‍👧 Caregiver collaboration \n📅 Smarter medication planning \n♿ Improved accessibility \n🤖 Expanded WebMCP capabilities with human approval \n\n Our goal is simple: \n\n SafeDose AI — Your regimen. Your agent. Safer decisions. 🛡️ \n\n Disclaimer: SafeDose is a prototype decision-support tool and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. \n\n \n\n \n Built With \n\n ai api github google html5 javascript openfda react studio typescript vercel webmcp \n \n\n \n Try it out \n\n \n \n \n safedose-ai.vercel.app \n \n \n \n GitHub Repo \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n \n \n Submitted to\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n The WebMCP Challenge \n \n\n\n \n\n\n \n\n \n \n\n \n \n Created by\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n Haseeb Ahmad \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n Designer, Database Specialist \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n Like\n 1 \n \n \n\n\n \n 1 person likes this:\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n Share this project:\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n \n \n \n Updates \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n Haseeb Ahmad \n started this project\n \n —\n \n a day ago \n \n \n\n
