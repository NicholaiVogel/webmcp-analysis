Have you tried installing skills, tools, or new workflows for your agent? Have you tried to share a skill you developed with your work colleagues? If you have you'll know why BeCapa is a must for you and your team. If you haven't, then you are missing out on the power of agents. \n\n What it does \n\n Becapa makes capability discovery easy. BeCapa makes secure sharing of enterprise developed skills secure and easy. It's your private app store for the enterprise. \n\n How we built it \n\n BeCapa was built with Codex. \n\n Challenges we ran into \n\n Speed. Once the problem was identified. It was running into speed boundaries at which point we've moved to parallel agent execution. Beyond development challenges, it's the cold start problem with starting a capabilities marketplace. You have none to start with, so where do we start? Then it's a marketing and acquisition problem. How do we get users? You can build something amazing, but if people don't know it exists, you have provided value to nobody but yourself. \n\n Accomplishments that we're proud of \n\n It's incredible to experience how easy tools, skills, and workflows are to install on any machine across all of your agents. I don't even use a companies docs for MCP tool installs anymore. I tell my agent to publish X in BeCapa and then I install the new capability using BeCapa. The workflow is insanely smooth. Not a single config file or text edit required by the user. \n\n What we learned \n\n Third party MCP servers, skills, and Ai capabilities should be a thing. If a company is too slow to develop for AI, why can't third parties do it on their behalf? To close the gap the way software hackers closed the gap decades ago until vendors caught up with their own tools. One reason is Apache licensing. Another is "reselling" of API laws that need clarity around is an MCP wrapper or business wrapper around an existing companies API's actually a violation of the API license? TBD...Nobody has challenged these yet. \n\n What's next for BeCapa \n\n We are growing our capabilities. We want more users, but we see the largest opportunity within the enteprrise to deploy their own private capabilities stores. We also see a great opportunity for third party publishers to run ahead of companies that aren't developing for Ai and monetize their agent capabilities. BeCapa will push the "Earn" portion of our offering to attract those third party developers. \n\n \n\n \n Built With \n\n codex firebase mcp webmcp \n \n\n \n Try it out \n\n \n \n \n BeCapa.com \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n \n \n Submitted to\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n The WebMCP Challenge \n \n\n\n \n\n\n \n\n \n \n\n \n \n Created by\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n Daniel Scarberry \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n Like\n \n \n\n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n Share this project:\n \n\n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n\n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n\n\n \n \n \n Updates \n\n \n \n \n \n \n\n \n \n \n \n \n \n Daniel Scarberry \n started this project\n \n —\n \n 10 days ago \n \n \n\n
