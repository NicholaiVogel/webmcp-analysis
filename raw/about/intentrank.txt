Agents change that. They can open a site, ask for a quote, and negotiate. They can also overshare: identity, vendor history, exact willingness to pay. \n\n I wanted a website that could complete a commercial handshake with an agent without turning the agent into a leaky CRM. WebMCP made that possible. The page registers a small tool contract. The agent calls those tools. The buyer decides what is disclosed. The human still has to say yes before anything consequential happens. \n\n What it does \n\n IntentRank is a consent-first procurement demo. A buyer’s agent does not scrape a page or dump a profile. It talks to the website through a narrow tool contract, shares only approved constraints, and can close a better deal only after the human says yes \n\n IntentRank is a consent-first procurement demo.
