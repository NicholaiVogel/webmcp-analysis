The issue could involve a client, KYC, or a transaction. I created WebMCP to help stakeholders investigate problems faster \n\n What it does \n\n Avenlo brings CRM investigation tools into one place. It helps teams search clients, review KYC information, and check transactions quickly. Each tool respects the user’s permissions \n\n How we built it \n\n I built Avenlo with Vue, PHP, MySQL, Docker, and Nginx. I connected WebMCP tools to my existing CRM data.
