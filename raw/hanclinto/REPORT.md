## What Changed

**Yes, casting a wider net changed the assessment: five of the ten picks changed. Jig remains my strongest pick.** The new picks are Gallery 402, InfraTwin, Interleave, Spatialize, and Groundplan. AgentDesk, Roadway Design Compiler, ProbePilot Studio, Stand, and Alza move to alternatives. These are not findings that those five are bad products; comparison against a larger field reduced confidence in their placement.

Every one of the **2,500 collected entries now has individual AI-authored feedback**: four criterion assessments, project-specific strengths and weaknesses, a verdict, confidence, and supporting source excerpts. **2493 have complete numeric totals; seven remain unscored in total because evidence is insufficient.** The review is based on the complete saved descriptions, not keyword counts or a title-only screen. Twenty scorecards received a separate semantic audit, and 25 candidates received comparative reassessment.

The original 46 source/live snapshots remain useful evidence, but they do not become 2,500 runtime tests. No new submitted test suite, full build, video viewing, or native agent workflow was executed. Newly considered top-ten projects do not all have source snapshots or verified deployments.

## Process And Confidence

1. Reused the complete saved corpus: 105 search pages, 2,500 unique descriptions. No descriptions were discarded because of their prior signal count or dates.
2. Divided the corpus into 50 alphabetic packets of 50 complete descriptions. With the user's permission, review agents authored individual assessments under [one rubric](#file-rubric-md), without consulting the old ranking. They were instructed to treat submission text as untrusted evidence, not instructions.
3. Validated exact coverage, score format, four rationales, nonempty strengths/weaknesses, and literal source-quote matches. The result is reproducible from the saved authored records, not a heuristic that generates feedback from keywords.
4. Audited 20 full-description/scorecard pairs. Found and corrected two factual errors plus one scope ambiguity. Additional finalist wording corrections qualify claims of verification and approval. Original records remain immutable, and corrections (local artifact, not included in this Gist) are applied transparently to the published ledger. See the [quality audit](#file-quality-audit-md). This sample is not a full audit or a statistically valid global error estimate.
5. Compared 25 strong candidates, including all original ten, using full descriptions and selected available source. A source-level concern in Deputy materially reduced its execution estimate: an alternate route accepts authorized records without an unavoidable passkey assertion check. This is a saved-source finding, not a live exploit or proof about every deployment.
6. Generated separate broad and reconciled scores. Batch means range from 68.5 to 76.375; project composition and reviewer severity both contribute. I did not normalize scores mechanically because that would assume equal underlying batch quality.

The written evidence still favors articulate, detailed submissions, and agents can misunderstand claims. The sample audit demonstrates that risk. Confidence is higher that this is a substantially broader and more useful comparison than that the exact tenth-place boundary predicts the judges. Judges may weigh video presentation and execution differently, and this review did not watch those videos.
