Allowing agents to order and interpret the results of a routine biochemical assay. Like Comment Story Updates "Notebook paired". This means a session exists between the lab notebook and chatGPT browser ClearSignal. The price from the website is quickly found and quoted via webMCP `quote_endotoxin_tests`. The lab notebook can receive status updates. Order ID available for confirmation. Results page shows actionable data and the standard curve. All in the lab notebook. Inspiration Life sciences labs sell services to other labs. One of those services is the endotoxin detection assay. An assay is a test. Endotoxin is a molecule from bacteria that can cause fevers in humans and other animals. Labs producing a product like a vaccine or a protein want to ensure their product is free of endotoxin. Detecting endotoxin is a routine, well-known biochemical assay that uses horseshoe crab blood or a protein from horseshoe crab blood. The problem is that this simple test is encumbered with difficulty. 1) A researcher who wishes an endotoxin test must search online for a provider. 2) The researcher "reaches out" through a site request form or by email or by phone calling another human. 3) The researcher and provider discuss terms: how many samples, how often, customization, etc. 4) The providers gets back in a few days with a quote. 5) The researcher agrees to the quote, signs it, sends it back. 6) The researcher prepares the samples and mails them 7) Samples are received and processed, usually 3-5 business days. 8) Provider sends email saying samples have arrived and are being processed. 9) Results are sent, usually by email, in a pdf. Fancy provider labs have the researchers create an account and sign in to download a pdf. 10) Researcher combs through 5-8 pages of lot numbers and temperature settings to find the 3 or 5 datapoints that are actionable, i.e. "Endotoxin < 0.05 EU/mL". I want to condense the above steps to one step: A researcher uses their lab notebook to order an endotoxin test. And then I want every lab in the world to join us. Every lab selling their specialized assay order-able, update-able and interpretable with webMCP-enabled sites. This is the Federated Cloud Laboratory Network. Materials Needed 1) chatGPT desktop with browser and webMCP enabled. 2) Obsidian, https://obsidian.md/ 3) Obisidian plugin, "ClearSignal Lab Assistant", https://github.com/har1eyk/clearsignal-lab-assistant 4) Lab notebook page for testing (any markdown file will do!) 5) ClearSignal access, https://clearsignal-gray.vercel.app/ 1) user: endo@toxin.com 2) pass: horseshoe What it does A researcher keeps a lab notebook in a markdown file. A markdown viewer like Obsidian is used. Obsidian can use plugins for beyond-markdown functionality. I created a plugin that allows a researcher to call for an endotoxin assay test from their lab notebook using three backticks. After the backticks, "clearsignal" is typed and a researcher can request: Order an endotoxin assay on two samples if the price is less than <$500. The sample IDs are: "ID-0001 and ID-0002". Then, the plugin begins a session with chatGPT's browser and the ClearSignal site. The webMCP-enabled site can easily find the endotoxin-per-sample-cost and report it. The quote is provided to the lab researcher in their notebook! Additionally, the researcher can also ask questions in their original prompt like: Can I send in plasmid samples? (Plasmids are circular pieces of DNA purified from bacteria.) The webMCP-enabled site answers it directly from the FAQ. No hunting and clicking for the page and the question. Save those tokens with webMCP. Lastly, the researcher's samples are loaded and the price is visible. The final "submit" creates the order. Order information is transmitted to the lab notebook. Status updates are also provided. In this demo version, status updates occur every few seconds. In real testing conditions, the status updates occur every few days. I like that when the testing is "complete", that endotoxin concentration and standard curve data is sent to the lab notebook so the researcher can take actionable next steps.
